A comprehensive audit typically examines site indexability, Core Web Vitals, on-page optimisation, internal linking, backlink profiles, and identifies competitor gaps for key search terms. Local Search vs. Broader Organic SEO

Local SEO is distinct from broader organic search efforts. It concentrates on enhancing visibility within Google Maps and local pack results for specific suburbs and service areas. This involves optimising Google Business Profile, local directory listings, and creating suburb-specific landing pages, alongside review strategies. In contrast, rebuilding e-commerce category pages or resolving site-wide crawl and indexation issues falls under broader organic SEO. For Brisbane-based service businesses, a key evaluation point is whether a proposed strategy clearly defines the type of search result it aims to influence. Visibility in maps, traditional blue-link results, product pages, and paid search all operate differently. While a plan may integrate these, it should articulate the role of each channel. Suburb references should be used judiciously, linked to actual service demand and supported by relevant content and internal links, rather than mere repetition.

Defining Retainer Scope and Visibility

A transparent SEO retainer should encompass keyword research, on-page tune-ups, link acquisition, and content creation, with monthly reporting that tracks completed work rather than just traffic metrics. This distinction is vital, as increased traffic does not always translate to more enquiries, and improved rankings may not align with buyer intent. Before agreeing to a monthly scope, it is advisable to understand the specific work units included in the fee. For instance, a technical SEO month might focus on crawlability, indexation, Core Web Vitals, and structured data. A content-focused month could involve developing keyword-mapped pages and internal linking strategies. Link building efforts might include digital PR, securing supplier mentions, or placing resource articles. The optimal mix of these activities should be dictated by the audit findings, rather than a one-size-fits-all approach. Published cost ranges indicate typical Brisbane retainers from $1,200 to $4,000+GST per month, with one-off audits and shorter campaigns starting around $800. These figures serve as a guide for scoping, not a fixed quote.
